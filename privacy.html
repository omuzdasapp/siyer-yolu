Siyer Yolu · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Developer: Yakup Işık (Saadet Evreni) · Contact: saadetevreni3@gmail.com

This is a translation of the Turkish version; in case of any difference, the Turkish version prevails.

In short

No ads, no purchases, no tracking. The lessons and your progress are stored only on your own device. Only if you use the live race are the nickname you choose and your race results kept on our server so that the race can work.

Information that stays on your device

Which lessons you have finished, your daily streak, your badges, your mode and language choice and your sound setting are stored in the browser's local storage (localStorage). This information is not sent to us. You can remove it with Settings → "Reset progress" or by uninstalling the app.

This data is stored on Supabase (Supabase Inc.) infrastructure, on servers in the European Union region, and is encrypted in transit. It is not shared with third parties, not sold and not used for advertising. There is no chat or messaging in the races.

Daily hadith notification

This feature is optional and does not work unless you turn it on. If you do, to deliver the notification to your phone we store on our server the notification address and encryption keys provided by the browser/phone, your chosen language, your phone's time zone and the notification time. Notifications are delivered through Google's notification infrastructure (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and their content is sent encrypted. When you turn it off in Settings → "Daily hadith notification", this record is deleted. This information is not used for any other purpose.

Deleting your account

In the app, Settings → "Delete my race account" permanently deletes your nickname, points and race answers. If you cannot access the app, write to saadetevreni3@gmail.com with your nickname; we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

In kids mode the live race is turned off; the child only plays against a computer opponent (bot) and no information is sent to the server. Leaving kids mode requires answering a parent question. Children under 13 should use the live race under parental supervision.

Read-aloud

The "Listen" feature plays recorded audio files from our website; if a recording is not available, your device's own speech engine is used. In both cases the texts are not sent to us and no information about you is collected.

Your rights

You can ask us for information about your data, its correction or deletion by writing to saadetevreni3@gmail.com. As a developer based in Türkiye, we process data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).
